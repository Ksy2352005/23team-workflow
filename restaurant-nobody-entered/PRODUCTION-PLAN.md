# The Restaurant Nobody Entered — Production Plan

**Client:** 23 Team
**Format:** Instagram Reel, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps
**Runtime:** 58.7 s (plus an optional 2 s end card)
**Voiceover:** `refs/voiceover-hu.mp3`, 56.2 s, used exactly as supplied

Scene timings come from the pauses in the voiceover file. Where a cut falls mid-sentence, it is marked "≈" and should be fine-tuned on the waveform in the edit.

## How to use this plan

1. **Image prompts**
   - Generate each one at 9:16.
   - Always attach `refs/character-sheet.png` as the character reference.
   - Also attach a matching panel from the Budapest world sheets as a style and environment reference.
   - Once Scene 2 and Scene 7 look right, reuse them as environment references for the interior and exterior scenes that follow, so the restaurant stays the same place.
2. **Animation prompts**
   - Feed each approved image into image-to-video as the first frame.
   - Generate 5 s or 10 s clips, then trim to the scene duration.
3. **Negative prompt for every generation:** `text, letters, words, signage, logos, watermark, subtitles, numbers, clock, extra fingers, hands with fingers, clothing, hair, eyebrows, nose, ears, realistic humans, skyscrapers, futuristic buildings, neon, cluttered props, distorted face, extra limbs, colour shift`
4. **Voice and sound:** the voiceover, music and sound effects are added in the edit. Generate the video clips without speech.

## Continuity bible

These descriptions are already written into every prompt below. Use this section to check results.

### Characters

**All characters** match the 23 Team mascot from the reference sheet:
- a simple rounded vinyl-toy figure with an oversized, perfectly round head;
- a small rounded body;
- short stubby arms with mitten hands and no fingers;
- short legs with rounded feet;
- two small glossy black oval eyes and a tiny thin black mouth;
- no nose, ears, hair, eyebrows or clothes;
- a smooth, soft matte surface in one solid colour.

**The cast:**

| Character | Colour | Role and rules |
|---|---|---|
| The owner | Vivid royal blue | The only blue character inside the restaurant. |
| First guest | Soft white | Always sits at the window table. |
| Passer-by | Warm yellow | Becomes the second guest. |
| Extra guests | Yellow, white and light grey | Never blue. |

**Expressions:** use only the four on the sheet: neutral (short line mouth), smile (open D-shaped smile), sad (small downturned mouth) and surprised (small round "o").

### The restaurant exterior

- A small corner restaurant on a cobblestone street in Budapest's old town.
- The building is a cream and pale-ochre neoclassical facade.
- A navy-and-cream striped awning hangs over the front.
- The front has a dark navy wooden door with a round brass handle on the left, and one large arched window on the right.
- A small brass bell hangs above the inside of the door.
- There are two wall lanterns and two potted olive trees out front.
- A yellow Budapest-style tram line runs at the far end of the street.

### The restaurant interior

- Honey-oak floorboards.
- Deep navy walls with cream wainscoting.
- Six small round oak tables in two rows, with navy-cushioned bentwood chairs.
- The window table sits right against the arched window.
- Globe pendant lamps give warm golden light.
- A short oak counter with a brass espresso machine stands at the back right.
- A few green potted plants.

### Light and look

- A premium, stylised 3D animated-film look.
- Deep royal blue and navy palette with warm golden lamp light.
- Soft shadows and gentle glow.
- Cinematic shallow depth of field.
- Clean, uncluttered frames.
