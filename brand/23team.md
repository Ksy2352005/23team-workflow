# 23 Team — brand profile

Read this before making any content, reels, graphics or copy for 23 Team.

## Identity
- **Name:** 23 Team (written "23 TEAM" in caps in the site header)
- **Location:** Budapest, Hungary. Works with clients in Hungary and internationally.
- **Website:** https://23team.hu (English, Hungarian, Arabic)
- **Instagram:** @23team.hu and @23team.marketing (the website's "DM us" button links to @23team.marketing)
- **Positioning:** "We Turn Businesses Into Brands" / "ONE TEAM. MANY SPECIALISTS."

## Services
- Websites: design, development, SEO, booking systems, e-commerce, payments, site management
- Photography, videography and content: photos, Reels, promo videos and ads, graphic design
- Graphic design and branding: logos, visual identities, menus, flyers, brochures, business cards, social graphics
- Social media marketing: strategy, content, planning, full management
- Advertising: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Google Ads, PPC, budget planning
- Clients can buy one service or a full-team package

## Voice
Direct, short, friendly, second person, benefit-led, little jargon.
Process: Talk → Plan → Create → Grow.
Lines in use: "Your business. Your goals. Your package." · "Stay visible, stay relevant and stay in people's minds."

## Branding showcase reels (planned content series)
- Fictional brand identities that show off 23 Team's branding skills.
- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 6–13 seconds, 8–12 shots cut on the beat.
- Signature look: the logo stays fixed in the centre while the scenes behind it change, and each scene is composed so the logo still sits correctly on the object (cup, bag, sign).
- Ending: brand awareness only, no call to action: the last picture of the edit (cut on the beat, same music) is the **23 logo** at the size and position locked in the 23motion films (324×177, centred, 75% opacity), coloured with the fictional brand's palette: darkest colour behind, lightest colour for the logo.
- Images: the user prefers OpenAI (ChatGPT) image generation.
- Music: chosen per reel to match the cuts. Instagram trending audio can also be added in the app when posting.
