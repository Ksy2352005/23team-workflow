Customers don't care about you.
They don't care about your product and they don't care about your brand.
The only thing they care about is themselves.
So if you want to sell more, simply show how you are all about them and how your product transforms their life.
Help them solve problems, reach goals, or feel amazing.
When you position your audience as the hero in their success story, your brand becomes their place to be.
