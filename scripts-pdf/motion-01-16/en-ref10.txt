This simple principle decides whether your marketing is great or bad.
If your brand looks average, people will assume it is average.
This is what's called the halo effect.
One strong impression, good or bad, influences directly everything that follows.
So if your design is weak, your offer feels weak.
If your site looks outdated, your product feels outdated.
The brain doesn't separate, it generalizes and transfers information.
So if you want to be seen as premium, make everything look premium.
Because in marketing, your product is defined by perception, not reality.
