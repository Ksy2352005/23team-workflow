What if I told you there's a key strategy that every successful business has in common?
It's called the marketing mix, and it's your ultimate playbook to getting your product in front of the right people.
The four P's, product, price, place, and promotion, are the foundation of any successful business strategy.
Product answers the question, what are you offering, and how does it solve a problem?
Price answers, how much is it worth to your customers, and does it match the value?
Place tells, where can your customers find it, and how do you make it accessible?
And promotion answers, how do you tell the world about it?
Align these four P's, and you've got the formula for business success.
