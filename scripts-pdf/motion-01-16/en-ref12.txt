In marketing, you need to understand this one thing.
The more people have to think, the less they trust you.
Not because they're lazy, but because the brain links confusion with risk.
If your message takes effort to understand, your offer feels risky.
And people don't buy risk.
They buy certainty.
This is why the clearest message always wins.
Not the most creative, not the most detailed, the clearest.
Because clarity is not just about words.
It is the feeling that you know exactly what you're doing.
