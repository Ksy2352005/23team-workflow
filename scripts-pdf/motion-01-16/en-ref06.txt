This is what you need to know for marketing to the masses.
People don't just want great products.
They want what everyone else wants.
Think about it.
Why do luxury brands limit supply?
Why do concert tickets sell out instantly?
Why does every iPhone launch feel urgent?
It's because of FOMO, the fear of missing out.
The second something feels limited, people want it more.
If you make your product feel exclusive, in demand, or limited, customers won't just want it, they will need it.
