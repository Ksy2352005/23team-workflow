As a business, you need to understand, people don't want to gain something.
They want to stop losing.
The fear of losing $100 is twice as powerful as the joy of gaining $100.
This is not a theory.
It's simply how our brain works.
So if your business only shows what people can get, you're missing half the equation.
Show them what they are already losing by not acting.
Because the pain of standing still is the most underused trigger in marketing.
