# MORZSA — fictional brand (23 Team concept)

- **Name:** "morzsa" is Hungarian for *crumb*. "Morzsi" is also one of the most classic Hungarian dog names, so the mascot is a cookie-stealing dachshund called **Morzsi**.
- **What:** a small Budapest cookie bakery (Bp. VIII.) selling big, gooey chocolate chip cookies, by the piece, in paper sleeves, in 6-boxes and in gift tins.
- **Audience:** young locals, students and visitors who queue for a warm cookie and want something that looks good on Instagram.
- **Logo:** lowercase "morzsa" in a fat, round, friendly typeface (Bagel Fat One). The "o" is a chocolate chip cookie with a bite taken out: the crumb that names the brand.
- **Palette (trendy chocolate + baby blue):** Chocolate `#3A1F14` · Baby blue `#A9CBEA` · Cream `#F6EEDF` · Biscuit `#D9984C` · Dark cocoa `#22120B`
  - Chocolate and biscuit are the product itself; baby blue makes it fresh and modern (and stands out against every brown bakery).
- **Type:** Bagel Fat One (logo, headlines: soft and chunky like a cookie) · DM Mono (small labels)
- **Lines:** "Crumbs worth fighting for." · "Big cookie energy." · "Baked fresh in Bp. VIII."
- **Mascot:** Morzsi the dachshund, always with a cookie in its mouth, drawn as simple line art with a cream fill.
- **Shots rule:** every photo features the cookies, except packaging that holds cookies (box, tin, sleeve) and the bakery itself. The opening monitor shot has a cookie on the desk.
- **Reel format:** Luwak-style desk reveal on `music/ref-luwak.mp4` (song ends 6.38 s; last real beat 6.13 s).
