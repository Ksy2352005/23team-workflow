# KOCKA — fictional brand (23 Team concept)

- **Name:** "kocka" is Hungarian for *cube*. A brownie is cut into cubes, so the name is the product.
- **What:** a premium brownie brand from Budapest: fudgy chocolate brownies with toppings (walnut, dark chocolate chips, pistachio, salted caramel, hazelnut, cocoa crumble), sold in a signature gift box of 12, a slim box of 4 and single wrapped pieces, in good supermarkets and delis.
- **Audience:** young shoppers and office snackers who want a treat that looks better than the usual chocolate aisle.
- **Logo (v3):** "KOCKA" in Cormorant Garamond caps with wide spacing, in gold foil on chocolate, under a monogram: a "K" in a double square frame with four corner dots (the square = the brownie cut).
- **Identity system (v3, after the user's Pinterest references):** luxury chocolate-brown packaging with gold foil lace corners and a framed central label; the open box shows 12 brownies with assorted toppings. The real brownies (crackly shiny top, fudgy inside, cocoa dust, chocolate drip) are the hero of every photo. Earlier versions rejected: cobalt (v1), plain white + brown (v2).
- **Palette:** Chocolate `#3A2218` · Dark cocoa `#24140D` · Gold foil `#C9A45C` · Cream `#F3EBDD` · Milk `#6B4430`
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (caps logo and headlines, italic taglines) · Inter Tight (small labels)
- **Lines:** "Signature brownies." · "Fudgy, dense, golden." · "Baked in Budapest."
- **Shots rule:** every photo features the brownie cubes or packaging holding them (gift box, box of 4, single wrap). No shop or café.
- **Reel format:** Luwak-style desk reveal on `music/ref-luwak.mp4` (song ends 6.38 s; drop about 3.45 s; last real beat 6.13 s). End card: cream 23 on dark cocoa `#24140D`.
