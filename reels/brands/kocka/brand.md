# KOCKA — fictional brand (23 Team concept)

- **Name:** "kocka" is Hungarian for *cube*. A brownie is cut into cubes, so the name is the product.
- **What:** a supermarket snack brand: fudgy chocolate brownie cubes, baked in Budapest. Sold as a stand-up pouch of 8 cubes, a box of 16, and single-wrapped cubes for lunchboxes.
- **Audience:** young shoppers and office snackers who want a treat that looks better than the usual chocolate aisle.
- **Logo:** lowercase "kocka" in Unbounded Black (wide, geometric, bold). The "o" is a brownie square with a crackly top: the cube in the name.
- **Identity system:** built on the brownie's square cuts: grids, squares, cube patterns, and a big square "o" used as a supergraphic. No mascot; the square is the character.
- **Palette:** Chocolate `#3B2016` · Cobalt `#2F5BEA` · Cream `#F4ECDD` · Caramel `#E0A458` · Deep cocoa `#1E0F09`
  - Chocolate and caramel are the brownie; cobalt makes the pack jump off a brown chocolate aisle.
- **Type:** Unbounded Black (logo, headlines) · DM Mono (labels, info)
- **Lines:** "Square up." · "Fudge-dense. Bite-size." · "Baked in Budapest."
- **Shots rule:** every photo features the brownie cubes or packaging holding them (pouch, box, single wrap, supermarket shelf). No shop or café.
- **Reel format:** Luwak-style desk reveal on `music/ref-luwak.mp4` (song ends 6.38 s; drop about 3.45 s; last real beat 6.13 s). End card: cream 23 on deep cocoa `#1E0F09`.
