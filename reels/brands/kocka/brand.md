# KOCKA — fictional brand (23 Team concept)

- **Name:** "kocka" is Hungarian for *cube*. A brownie is cut into cubes, so the name is the product.
- **What:** a supermarket snack brand: fudgy chocolate brownie cubes, baked in Budapest. Sold as a stand-up pouch of 8 cubes, a box of 16, and single-wrapped cubes for lunchboxes.
- **Audience:** young shoppers and office snackers who want a treat that looks better than the usual chocolate aisle.
- **Logo:** lowercase "kocka" in Gloock (a warm, chunky serif) next to the mark: a rounded chocolate square with two crackle lines, a brownie cube seen from above.
- **Identity system (v2, white + chocolate):** premium and appetising. White matte packs with a melted-chocolate lower half or a big chocolate panel, the brownie cube as hero, a cut-grid graphic. No mascot.
- **Palette:** White `#FFFFFF` · Dark chocolate `#3A2218` · Milk chocolate `#8B5A3C` · Cocoa dust `#CDB49C` · Ink `#1C110C` (the first cobalt version was rejected: blue doesn't suit a brownie).
- **Type:** Gloock (logo, headlines) · Instrument Serif Italic (taglines) · Inter Tight (small labels)
- **Lines:** "Square up." · "Fudge-dense. Bite-size." · "Baked in Budapest."
- **Shots rule:** every photo features the brownie cubes or packaging holding them (pouch, box, single wrap, supermarket shelf). No shop or café.
- **Reel format:** Luwak-style desk reveal on `music/ref-luwak.mp4` (song ends 6.38 s; drop about 3.45 s; last real beat 6.13 s). End card: white 23 on ink `#1C110C`.
