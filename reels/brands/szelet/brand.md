# SZELET — fictional brand (23 Team concept)

- **Name:** "szelet" is Hungarian for *slice*.
- **What:** a supermarket frozen stone-baked pizza brand. Four flavours in square boxes: Margherita, Pepperoni, Funghi, Four Cheese.
- **Audience:** young city people who want a frozen pizza that looks fun and tastes like a pizzeria.
- **Logo:** lowercase "szelet" in Bagel Fat One (fat, round, bouncy), with a pizza-slice mark tilted at the end like a flag.
- **Palette (pop, colour-blocked):** Tomato red `#FF3B1F` · Basil green `#2DBE4E` · Mozzarella cream `#FFF4DC` · Sky blue `#6FD3FF` · Sunny yellow `#FFD21F` · Crust brown `#8A3B12`
  - One colour per flavour: Margherita red, Pepperoni yellow, Funghi green, Four Cheese blue.
- **Type:** Bagel Fat One (logo, headlines) · DM Mono (labels)
- **Lines:** "Slice of the good life." · "Stone-baked. Freezer-ready." · "Pull it, share it."
- **Shots rule:** every photo shows the pizza or the SZELET boxes.
- **Reel format:** tile-mosaic transitions (reference `music/ref-3.mp4`, 185 BPM, 6.45 s): 5 photos, the screen split into a 3 × 5 grid whose tiles flip to the next photo one by one on the beat, then the 23 card.
