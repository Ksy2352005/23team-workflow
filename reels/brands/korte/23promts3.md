## Look (every image)
Editorial lifestyle photography for a design-studio branding case study, like a Behance project:
- **Real places and real hands**: café counters, wooden tables, sunny Budapest streets, simple studio sets in the brand colours.
- **Natural daylight**, soft but with real shadows, warm and fresh. True-to-life colour, slight film grain.
- **The brand is the hero**: KÖRTE colours everywhere (burgundy #5B1A2E, butter yellow #F3D35B, cream #F5EEDF, pear green), nothing else competing.
- Close, confident framing; products sharp and easy to read.

## Avoid (every image)
Dark moody bar scenes, black backgrounds, bokeh light balls, lens flare, water droplets, splashes, mint leaves, oranges or citrus, glossy 3D render or CGI look, stock-advert style, clutter, any other brand, any text not in the brand kit.

## Composition (every image)
Keep the main subject in the **middle 75% of the width**; the sides will be cropped to 9:16.
