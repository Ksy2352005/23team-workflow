# KÖRTE — fictional brand (23 Team concept)

- **What:** a Budapest café (Bp. VII.) built around its own canned sparkling pear drinks, cold pressed, no sugar added. You drink a KÖRTE can at the café or take a 4-pack home.
- **Audience:** young locals and visitors who want something local, natural and good-looking on the table instead of sugary soda.
- **Mascot:** Körti, a pear whose eyes are the two seeds from the logo.
- **Shots rule:** every photo features the canned drink, except the 4-pack packaging (with cans) and the café itself.
- **Name:** "körte" is Hungarian for pear.
- **Logo:** lowercase "körte" in Fraunces Black (soft, wonky cut). The two umlaut dots over the ö are pear seeds.
- **Palette:** Burgundy `#5B1A2E` · Butter `#F3D35B` · Cream `#F5EEDF` · Deep burgundy `#3E0F1E`
- **Type:** Fraunces Black (display), JetBrains Mono (small caps labels)
- **Lines:** "Pear, but make it fizz." · "Drink the orchard." · "Cold pressed in Budapest."
