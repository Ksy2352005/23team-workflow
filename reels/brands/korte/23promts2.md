## Brand
- **KÖRTE** means "pear" in Hungarian. Sparkling pear drink from Budapest, cold pressed, no sugar.
- **The fruit is PEAR.** Yellow-green pears only. No oranges, lemons, mint or other fruit.
- **Colours (use only these, plus natural pear green):**
  - Deep burgundy #5B1A2E (cans, cups, backdrops)
  - Butter yellow #F3D35B (backdrops)
  - Cream #F5EEDF (backdrops, paper, fabric)

## Look (every image)
Modern design-studio product photography, the kind used in branding portfolio posts:
- **Clean seamless colour backdrops** in the brand colours (one or two colours per image), colour-blocked, graphic.
- **Bright daylight or a soft studio light with one crisp, defined shadow.** Matte surfaces. Calm and minimal.
- **One hero product, close up**, lots of empty space around it, perfectly straight-on and symmetrical.
- Products are **matte, solid colour, plain**, like an unprinted mockup.
- Slight film grain, true-to-life colour, sharp focus everywhere.

## Avoid (every image)
Dark moody bar scenes, black backgrounds, bokeh lights, lens flare, water droplets and condensation, splashes, mint leaves, oranges, citrus, ice cubes, plants in the background, glossy 3D render or CGI look, "stock advert" style, clutter, extra props, any text.

## Composition (every image)
- The product's **blank label or flat surface is exactly in the centre** of the image, facing the camera straight on.
- That blank area is about **60% of the image width**.
- Keep everything important in the **middle 75% of the width**; the sides will be cropped to 9:16.
